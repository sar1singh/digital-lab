# ThinkLate Content & Retention Auditor Skill

## Purpose
Architects the psychological tension and narrative pacing of ThinkLate documentaries to sustain YouTube audience retention above 50% through the end screen.

## The 6-Minute Retention Curve Architecture

```
100% ────┐
         │ 00:00 [The Personal Hook: Viewer's workplace & salary reality]
 80%     └───┐
             │ 01:30 [The Magnitude Peak: Staggering 15-20% GDP capital scale]
 65%         └───┐
                 │ 02:45 [The Paradox Twist: Darwin & Mill wiped out despite clear data]
 55%             └───┐
                     │ 03:45 [The Invariant Payoff: Tracks survived, shareholders didn't]
 50%                 └───────┐ 05:00 [The Modern Mirror: AI Capex vs Headcount]
                             └─── 06:00 [The Lingering Close into silence]
```

## Retention Checkpoints
1. **The 30-Second Retention Gate:**
   - Must present a direct dilemma connected to the viewer's career/life before any history is introduced.
2. **Pacing Peaks Every 45–60 Seconds:**
   - Alternate between forensic document reveals, historical anecdotes, and conceptual mental models.
3. **Zero Algorithmic Begging:**
   - Never interrupt narrative immersion with spoken requests to like, subscribe, or ring bells.
   - The visual subscribe pop-up at Scene 8 occurs silently over continuous narration.
4. **The "Lunch Table" Test:**
   - The viewer must walk away with one crisp, indelible concept they can explain to a peer in two sentences.
