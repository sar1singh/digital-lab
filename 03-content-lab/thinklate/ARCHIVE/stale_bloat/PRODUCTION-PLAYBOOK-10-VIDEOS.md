## ⚖️ 2. The 8-Pillar Scene Quality Audit Rubric

Every visual asset—whether from our Custom Kit or NotebookLM—is audited against these 8 pillars (Total: 100 pts):

1. **Audience Retention Psychology (15 pts):** Sustains curiosity loops; eliminates "homework" friction; avoids cognitive overload.
2. **Data Point Dramatization (15 pts):** Isolates large metrics (*"15% to 20% of GDP"*, *"$600 Billion"*) in visceral, bold typography; passes the 1.5-second glance test.
3. **Narrative Flow & Transitions (15 pts):** Smooth entry/exit; 0.3s dip to navy or slide; prevents the 3-second stagnation trap without frantic cutting.
4. **Cadence & Semantic Sync (15 pts):** Highlight snaps or card reveals hit **on the exact stressed word** of narration.
5. **Primary Source Authority (10 pts):** Prioritizes authentic historical scans and official reports over generic AI re-summaries.
6. **Multi-Screen Legibility (10 pts):** EBU R95 120px title-safe margins; crisp legibility on a 6.1" smartphone at 360p and a 55" living room TV.
7. **Brand Signature Cohesion (10 pts):** Strict palette lock (`#1B2A4A` navy, `#D99A2B` amber, `#F4F1EA` paper); clean space for the Host Avatar.
8. **Editorial Execution Cost (5 pts):** Assembles in DaVinci in under 3 minutes using basic editing skills (position keyframes, zoom, cross dissolve).

## 🧠 3. Decision Matrix: Custom Plate vs. NotebookLM Asset

* **Use Custom ThinkLate Plate When:**
  * Delivering a punchy channel thesis or invariant (*"The tracks survived. The shareholders didn't"*).
  * Establishing the channel persona with the Host Avatar.
  * Showcasing the authentic primary source scan with physical highlighting.
* **Use NotebookLM Asset When:**
  * Explaining multi-step cause-and-effect relationships (Mind Maps / Infographics).
  * Presenting comparative financial data tables.
  * Breaking up long 40-second monologues with dynamic animated overview sequences.
* **The Hybrid Cut (Gold Standard):**
  * Start on Host/Custom Plate (10s) ➔ Cut to NotebookLM diagram (15s) ➔ Cut back to Host punchline (10s).

---

## 🔄 4. The 10-Video Iterative Retrospective Cycle

At the end of every episode production, execute `RETROSPECTIVE.md` to record:
1. **Audit Logs:** Which scenes used Custom vs NotebookLM, and their retention scores.
2. **DaVinci Timeline Speed:** Record editing hours and friction points to reduce assembly time on Episode N+1.
3. **Visual Evolution:** What new background textures, kinetic templates, or sound effects get promoted into the permanent channel library.
4. **Audience Feedback Integration:** What comments/drop-off points were noted on YouTube analytics to adjust the next script.
