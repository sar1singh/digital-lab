Is the multi-hundred-billion-dollar AI infrastructure buildout an unprecedented economic bubble—or a necessary historical phase? To find out, we have to look back to 1845. 

In the 1840s, Great Britain poured between 15% and 20% of its national GDP into constructing thousands of miles of railroad track. Promoters promised infinite guaranteed profits. Intellectual giants like Charles Darwin and John Stuart Mill gambled their life savings.
