# ThinkLate UI/UX Design Expert Audit Skill

## Purpose
Maintains broadcast-grade visual elegance, consistency, and typographic excellence across all visual deck cards, charts, and lower-third overlays.

## Design System Tokens & Rules
1. **Brand Palette Lock:**
   - **Background Canvas:** Deep Slate Navy (`#1B2A4A` / `#0D1524`) — pure black `#000` is strictly forbidden.
   - **Accent & Focus:** Warm Amber Gold (`#D99A2B`) — used for progress bars, focus rings, and key quote callout rules.
   - **Body Typography:** Warm Off-White / Paper (`#F4F1EA`) — comfortable high contrast without retina strain.
   - **Muted Metadata:** Slate Gray (`#98A2B3`) — for timestamps, secondary author affiliations.
2. **The 10-Foot TV Legibility Test:**
   - All text must remain effortlessly readable on a 55" living room television from 10 feet away.
   - Minimum font equivalent: 48px for main headlines, 28px for document titles, 22px for citations.
3. **Title-Safe Margins (EBU R95):**
   - Keep all critical cards, graphs, and badges at least **120px away** from the 1920x1080 canvas borders to avoid clipping on TVs and mobile notch displays.
4. **Information Architecture Stack:**
   - Every citation card follows: `[SOURCE CATEGORY PILL]` ➔ `[PRIMARY DOCUMENT TITLE]` ➔ `[AUTHOR & INSTITUTION]` ➔ `[EXACT VERIFIED CLAIM]`.
