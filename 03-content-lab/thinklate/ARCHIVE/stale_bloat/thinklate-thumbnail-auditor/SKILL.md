# ThinkLate Thumbnail & Packaging Auditor Skill

## Purpose
Maximizes organic YouTube click-through rate (CTR) among mature knowledge workers (ages 22–40) while maintaining documentary prestige and intellectual credibility.

## The 4 Core Evaluation Rules
1. **The 3-Second Glance Rule:**
   - Does a user scrolling rapidly on a mobile phone immediately grasp the core conflict or tension in under 3 seconds?
2. **The 120px Mobile Simulation Test:**
   - Downscale the thumbnail to 120px height (standard YouTube mobile app browse feed).
   - Are the primary subject and text overlay instantly legible without squinting?
3. **Curiosity Gap Over Title Duplication:**
   - Never restate the full video title on the thumbnail.
   - Limit overlay text to **2 to 4 punchy words** that pose a sharp dilemma:
     - Example: `REAL, BUT RUINED` / `IS AI NEXT?`
4. **Cinematic Documentary Realism:**
   - Strictly zero cartoonish AI illustrations, zero exaggerated YouTube reaction shock faces.
   - Visual tone must evoke prestige editorial publications (e.g. Bloomberg Originals, Financial Times, BBC Storyville).
5. **Bottom-Right Badge Clearance:**
   - Ensure critical graphics and text do not sit in the bottom-right corner where YouTube renders the video duration timestamp.
