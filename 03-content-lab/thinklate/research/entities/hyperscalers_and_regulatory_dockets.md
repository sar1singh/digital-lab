## 1. Regulatory Dockets & Statutory Records

### <a id="puco-24-508"></a>PUCO Docket No. 24-508-EL-ATA (AEP Ohio Tariff)
* **Jurisdiction:** Public Utilities Commission of Ohio (PUCO)
* **Parties:** AEP Ohio (Utility) vs. Data Center Coalition vs. Ohio Consumers' Counsel (OCC)
* **Core Issue:** Transmission cost socialization. Data centers requested 30,000+ MW in central Ohio. Standard rules allowed utilities to socialise substation upgrades across all residential ratepayers.
* **The Ruling:** Mandated that large compute facilities guarantee **85% capacity uptake in upfront collateral** to protect residential meters from abandoned buildouts.

### <a id="ferc-nuclear"></a>FERC Susquehanna Nuclear Co-Location Rejection
* **Docket:** Federal Energy Regulatory Commission Docket No. ER24-2172-000
* **Parties:** Talen Energy / Amazon AWS vs. PJM Interconnection & Regional Utilities (Exelon, AEP)
* **Core Issue:** Behind-the-meter diversion. Amazon contracted to purchase 960 MW directly from the 2.5 GW Susquehanna nuclear plant, bypassing the public transmission grid.
* **The Ruling:** FERC rejected the amended interconnection service agreement, citing grid reliability risks and unfair transmission cost shifts onto surrounding public customers.

### <a id="ca-ab2426"></a>California AB 2426 (Digital Consumer Disclosures)
* **Author:** Assemblymember Jacqui Irwin (Signed by Governor Gavin Newsom, September 2024)
* **Effective Date:** 2025
* **Core Mandate:** Prohibits digital sellers from using the terms "buy", "purchase", or any term a reasonable person would interpret as conferring ownership, unless the seller either holds a permanent non-revocable license or clearly provides a plain-language disclosure warning that the digital good can be revoked at any time.

### PJM Interconnection
* **Role:** Regional Transmission Organization (RTO) coordinating electricity across 13 Eastern/Midwestern states (65 million people).
* **Key Mechanism:** Base Residual Auction (clears wholesale capacity prices 3 years in advance). In the 2025/2026 auction, capacity clearing prices exploded from **\$28.92 to \$333.60/MW-day (+833%)** due to accelerating AI server farm load and coal plant retirements.
