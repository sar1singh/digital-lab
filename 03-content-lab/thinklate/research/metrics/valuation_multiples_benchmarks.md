## 2. Energy, Grid & Thermodynamic Equivalences

* **1 Gigawatt (GW):**
  * = 1,000 Megawatts (MW)
  * = 1,000,000 Kilowatts (kW)
  * = Power for ~750,000 average US residential households continuously.
* **Modern AI Chip Power Consumption:**
  * Nvidia H100 GPU: ~700 Watts (TDP)
  * Nvidia B200 / Blackwell GPU: ~1,000 to 1,200 Watts
  * Blackwell NVL72 Server Rack: ~120 kW to 132 kW per single rack (vs. 7 kW to 10 kW for conventional cloud racks).
* **Biological Compute (Human Brain):**
  * ~20 Watts of continuous biological power (glucose/ATP).
  * Executes multimodal perception, generalized spatial reasoning, humor, natural language, and full motor kinetics.
