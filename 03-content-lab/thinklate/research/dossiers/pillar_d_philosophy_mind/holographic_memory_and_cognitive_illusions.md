## 1. Core Paradox & Narrative Thesis
Human beings naively believe that when they recall their 10th birthday, their brain opens an MP4 video or a JPEG file from an anatomical hard drive folder. 

In neurobiology and cognitive philosophy, that is completely false: **Every single human memory is a dynamically reconstructed hallucination.** Following Dennis Gabor's 1948 mathematical physics of optical holography and Karl Pribram's holonomic brain theory, information is smeared non-locally across trillions of synaptic connection weights. Recalling a memory doesn't "read" it; it **re-simulates** it in real-time. Each time you access a memory, your brain alters it, overwrites it, and saves a copy of the copy.
