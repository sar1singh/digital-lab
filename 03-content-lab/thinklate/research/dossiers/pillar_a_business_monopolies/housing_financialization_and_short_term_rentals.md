## 1. Core Paradox & Narrative Thesis
Airbnb was born in October 2007 out of raw desperation: two broke art-school design grads facing an overdue \$1,100 rent bill who inflated three airbeds on their living room rug. 

Fast forward to 2026: the anti-hotel rebel transformed into the very monopoly monster it promised to kill—extracting **141% junk-fee markups**, forcing guests to complete 8-step chore checklists, locking out local renters across top 25 metropolitan areas, and triggering citywide municipal moratoria from New York to Barcelona.

## 3. The 3-Stage Monopoly Lifecycle (The ThinkLate Arc)
1. **The Asymmetric Infiltrator (Stage 1):** Unlicensed, zero physical asset overhead, subsidizing hosts, bypassing municipal occupancy hotel taxes.
2. **The De Facto Monopoly (Stage 2):** Locking in two-sided network effects; hotel supply is rigid and takes 4 years to build, while software platforms add 10,000 rooms with zero construction debt.
3. **The Junk-Fee Extractor (Stage 3):** Platform decay (Cory Doctorow's *Enshittification*): adding cleaning fees, service fees, local taxes, host chores, and algorithmic price gouging until the platform is more expensive and stressful than a standard hotel.
