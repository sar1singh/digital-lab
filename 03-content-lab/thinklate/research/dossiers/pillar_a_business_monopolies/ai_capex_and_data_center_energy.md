## 3. Regulatory Dockets & Legal Mechanisms

### A. The "Rate-Basing" Loophole (Corporate Welfare Mechanism)
* **How It Works:** Regulated electric utilities are legally guaranteed a **9% to 10% Return on Equity (ROE)** on all physical transmission assets they build.
* **The Incentive:** Power companies do not maximize profit by selling cheap electricity; they maximize profit by building enormous \$10B+ high-voltage transmission lines.
* **The Socialization:** Regulators allow utilities to add the capital cost of connecting AI campuses into the general rate base, spreading the cost across all captive residential meters rather than billing the private data center operator.

### B. PUCO Docket No. 24-508-EL-ATA (AEP Ohio Tariff)
* **The Dispute:** AEP Ohio was overwhelmed by 30+ GW of data center requests in central Ohio.
* **The Regulatory Ruling:** Required hyperscale data center operators to provide **85% upfront financial collateral** for requested capacity to ensure that if the AI boom collapses, residential ratepayers aren't left paying for stranded transmission lines.

### C. FERC Susquehanna Nuclear Co-Location Rejection (Nov 2024 / 2025)
* **The Case:** Talen Energy / Amazon AWS deal to siphon 960 MW of power directly from the Susquehanna nuclear power station "behind-the-meter".
* **The Ruling:** FERC rejected the amended interconnection agreement, ruling that diverting baseload nuclear power exclusively to a private data center would increase congestion and costs for everyday grid customers.
