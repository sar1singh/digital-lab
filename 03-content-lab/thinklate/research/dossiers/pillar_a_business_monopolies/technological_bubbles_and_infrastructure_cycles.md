## 1. Core Paradox & Historical Pattern (Carlota Perez Framework)
Every major technological revolution follows an identical **two-phase financial arc**:
1. **The Installation / Irrationally Exuberant Phase:** Speculative capital floods the infrastructure layer. Massive overcapacity is built (thousands of miles of parallel railway tracks, millions of miles of dark fiber-optic cables, gigawatts of AI compute). Early investors get wiped out.
2. **The Deployment / Post-Crash Utility Phase:** The bankrupt assets are bought pennies on the dollar, and the true economic productivity revolution begins 10–15 years later.

## 2. Hard Historical Benchmarks & Parallels

| Era / Technology | The Bubble Apex | The Capital Destruction | The Long-Term Reality |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **British Railway Mania (1844–1847)** | Parliament approved 8,500 miles of track; railway investment reached 7% of British GDP. | Index collapsed 65% by 1850. Bank of England forced to suspend Bank Charter Act. Over half of railway projects were abandoned. | The surviving network laid the physical foundation for the Victorian industrial empire. (Andrew Odlyzko research). |
| **Telecom & Fiber Boom (1998–2002)** | \$500 Billion poured into trans-oceanic fiber optic cables (Global Crossing, WorldCom). | 95% of dark fiber sat unlit in the ground. Over \$2 Trillion in market cap erased. Global Crossing & WorldCom went bankrupt. | The cheap unlit fiber subsidized the entire Web 2.0 and streaming revolution (YouTube, Netflix, AWS) 5 years later. |
| **Generative AI Buildout (2023–2026)** | Hyperscaler capex tracking over \$830B annualized; Nvidia datacenter revenue 10x in 24 months. | Sequoia's "$600B AI Question" & Goldman Sachs report: End-user AI revenue falls short of data center depreciation by hundreds of billions. | Power grid bottlenecks and ratepayer revolts force capex contraction; utility and localized deployment emerge post-hangover. |
