## 1. Core Paradox & Narrative Thesis
Why is the speed of light ($c \approx 299,792\text{ km/s}$) finite? 
In everyday pop-science, people treat the speed of light as a "maximum speed limit for spaceships." 

In theoretical physics, that is completely backwards. **$c$ is not a travel speed limit; it is the clock rate and refresh rate of the universe's causal rendering engine.** If $c$ were infinite, cause and effect would happen everywhere simultaneously: all stars would dump their energy instantly, time would collapse into a single point, and information could never exist. The universe has a hardware clock speed, and we live inside its physical rendering budget.
