## 3. How to Use Visual Evidence in Video Production (The Two-Track Law)

1. **The Primary Evidence Slam (0:00 – 0:08 Hook):**
   * Instead of starting with generic stock video of a person looking at a computer, slam the **actual 1948 Dennis Gabor patent diagram** or the **actual 1845 Punch magazine cartoon** onto Track 4 with a camera shutter SFX (`camera_vintage_shutter.mp3`) and yellow grease-pencil highlighter.
2. **The "Look With Your Own Eyes" Anchor:**
   * When the narrator says: *"This isn't a theory. In May of 1908, the Supreme Court wrote this exact sentence..."*, the screen displays the actual unredacted Supreme Court archival folio with the key phrase enlarged.
3. **Bidirectional Archival Adding:**
   * Whenever a video sources a high-resolution archival press photograph, a public domain patent plate, or a clean SVG scientific diagram, save it permanently to `thinklate/research/visual_evidence/` and register it in this ledger so future videos can pull it instantly.
