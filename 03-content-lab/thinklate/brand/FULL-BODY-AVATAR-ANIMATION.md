## ⚡ 4. How to Animate the Full-Body Avatar with AI Video

There are two primary methods to produce smooth, lip-synced animated A-roll video:

### METHOD 1: Audio-Driven Lip-Sync & Body Animation (Recommended)
* **Best Tools:** **Hedra** (`hedra.com`), **LivePortrait**, **Runway Act-One**, or **D-ID / HeyGen**.
* **How It Works:**
  1. Upload your generated full-body or half-body ThinkLate Owl image.
  2. Upload your recorded human voice take (`take_01.wav`).
  3. The engine automatically animates the character's beak, mouth shapes (visemes), eyes (blinks, focus), head movement, and natural chest breathing in **1:1 synchronization with your spoken words**.
* **Result:** 100% natural conversational timing with zero robotic drift.

---

### METHOD 2: Image-to-Video (I2V) in Google Vids / Google Flow / Runway / Kling
* **Best Tools:** **Google Vids**, **Google Flow**, **Runway Gen-3 Alpha (I2V)**, **Kling AI**, **Luma Dream Machine**.
* **How It Works:**
  1. Set the Full-Body Owl image as the **First Frame / Starting Reference**.
  2. Enter the motion prompt below.
  3. The AI generates a 5–10 second video clip of the character speaking and gesturing with broadcast anime motion.
