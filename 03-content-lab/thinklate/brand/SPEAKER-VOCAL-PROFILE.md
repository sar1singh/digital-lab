## 🔍 2. Why & Where You Fumbled / Stumbled

The analysis shows that **your voice does not fumble when you speak naturally**. Every stumble in Video 1 was triggered by **faulty script construction**:

### 🚫 Trap 1: The "Quote Unquote" Academic Citation Trap (Take 4 & 10)
* **What broke:** The script literally had `calls it quote Unquote. Greatest. In all of history.` and `Odlyzko writes that it had simply quote`.
* **Vocal Result:** You paused for 1.60 seconds, your cadence crashed from 175 WPM down to 97 WPM, and you had 13 hesitations in a 33-second take.
* **Rule for CEO / Scripting:** **BANNED.** Never write "quote/unquote". Paraphrase the idea in your own conversational words:
  * *Bad:* `"Professor Andrew Odlyzko of the University of Minnesota calls it quote..."`
  * *Good:* `"A researcher at Minnesota looked at the numbers and called it the single greatest bubble in modern history."`

### 🚫 Trap 2: The Multi-Unit Currency Tongue-Twister (Take 5)
* **What broke:** Reading `"three hundred to four hundred billion pounds, or three to four trillion dollars"`.
* **Vocal Result:** Cadence slowed to 103 WPM with 16 micro-stops.
* **Rule for CEO / Scripting:** Use single, round punchy comparisons:
  * *Good:* `"In today's cash, that's four trillion dollars handed over in physical checks."`

### 🚫 Trap 3: The Front-Loaded Adrenaline Rush (Take 1 & 2)
* **What broke:** Taking only 2 breaths in 14 seconds at 166 WPM.
* **Vocal Result:** Words blur together slightly in the first 15 seconds.
* **Rule for Recording:** **Take a full belly breath before pressing Record.** Deliver the first sentence like you're leaning over a coffee table telling a friend a secret, not reading a teleprompter countdown.

## ⚙️ 4. Standing Rules for Phase 4 (Scripting) & Phase 5 (Recording)

1. **Sentence Length Ceiling:** Maximum **12 words per breath**. If a sentence has two commas, split it into two sentences.
2. **No Academic Jargon:** Multi-syllable names and book titles get dropped or simplified.
3. **Pacing Target on Recording:** **135 WPM**. (Approximately 2.25 words per second).
4. **Breath Markers in Script:** The script must explicitly contain `//` (half-beat pause) and `///` (full beat dramatic pause) so you never run out of oxygen.
