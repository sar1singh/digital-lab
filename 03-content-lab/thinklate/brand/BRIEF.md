| | |
|---|---|

| **Why** | The avatar renders as a 32–48 px circle, and the channel is faceless. The mark is the only face-like signal available (`CRAFT.md` → PART `12_CRAFT-EVIDENCE` §2 — 69% of breakout videos use a face, 80% of top performers) |

## THE BRIEF — paste this whole block

```
You are an expert brand identity designer. Design a distinctive logo mark
for ThinkLate, a YouTube channel and media company.

THE SUBJECT IS AN OWL. This is settled — do not propose another creature.
The owl is chosen deliberately: in idiom and fiction it already means
wisdom, libraries, knowledge, the one who knows. That instant recognition
is an asset, not a cliché to escape.

THE PROBLEM TO SOLVE
I have rejected four rounds of owl logos. Every one was competent and
completely forgettable: a front-facing, symmetrical owl head, two round
eyes, a small beak. That is every owl logo ever made.

So the distinctiveness must come from HOW THE OWL IS DRAWN, not from
changing the animal. Be genuinely inventive with the drawing itself:

- Crop it hard. Let the face fill the frame and run off the edges
- Try profile, three-quarter, or looking back over its shoulder
- Break the symmetry. One eye larger, or only one eye visible
- Push the proportions somewhere unexpected
- Let negative space do the work — the owl defined by what is absent
- Show the geometric construction, or hide it completely
- Consider a woodcut, letterpress, or stencil quality

Pick ONE bold idea per concept and commit to it fully. A weird owl
executed with total conviction beats a correct owl every time.

WHAT IT IS FOR
ThinkLate explains how work, money and power actually function — economics,
geopolitics, technology — and how those forces reach one person's working
life. Long-form, researched, faceless. Every claim sourced, every inference
labelled. The name means hindsight: understanding that arrives after the
event, which is when understanding usually arrives.

Audience: Indian knowledge workers, 22 to 40, considering going
independent. Educated, sceptical, allergic to being sold to.

FEELING
Professional but relaxed. Confident without shouting. Looks like it knows
things and is not trying to impress anyone. Warm, not corporate. It should
have a slight attitude — a knowing quality, like it has an opinion.

HARD CONSTRAINTS — functional, not stylistic
- No text, letters, words or numbers anywhere
- LIGHT OWL ON A DARK FIELD. High contrast is mandatory — this is a
  faceless channel competing in a dark-mode feed
- Must still read as an owl at 32 x 32 pixels
- Flat colour only: no gradient, glow, 3D, bevel, shadow, texture or
  internal shading. Two tones plus the accent, maximum
- One accent colour only, used sparingly — ideally the eyes
- Fits inside a circle without cropping; it becomes a circular avatar
- Head only, or head and shoulders. No full bodies, no held props,
  no signboards
- Not cute, not a cartoon mascot, not a startup blob

COLOUR
Warm off-white #F4F1EA owl on deep indigo #1B2A4A, amber #D99A2B accent.

DELIVER
Six owls that look nothing like each other — six different drawing
approaches, not six variations of one. Flat vector, centred.
For each one, ALSO show it rendered at 32x32 pixels beside the large
version, so I can see what actually survives.
```

## Workflow

1. Full brief → a platform with a long prompt window (Gemini, ChatGPT, Leonardo, Firefly)
2. Short version → Bing, for a second opinion
3. Screenshot the grid, upload in chat
4. **Claude runs `QC.md` only.** Save the PNG into `brand/` and Claude also renders the real 32 px and contrast tests
5. Iterate by describing **what feels wrong**, never by prescribing geometry
6. Final vector cleanup and file prep in Canva or Figma (both free)
