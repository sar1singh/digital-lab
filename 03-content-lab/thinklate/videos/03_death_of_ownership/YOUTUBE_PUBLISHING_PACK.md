## 🎨 3. Design Tokens & YouTube Studio Upload Instructions

### The 3-Element Visual Rule:
1. **Focal Subject:** Maximum visual tension (e.g. empty physical disc tray vs $80 paper slip, or digital padlock clamped on Blu-ray disc).
2. **Contrast Palette:**
   - Base Shadow: Deep navy slate (`#0B132B` / `#1B2A4A`)
   - High-Voltage Warning: Neon crimson (`#EF4444`)
   - Data Accent: Warm amber gold (`#F59E0B`)
3. **Typography Rule:** Maximum 2 to 3 words. Use high-impact bold sans-serif (Bebas Neue / Montserrat ExtraBold) with dark drop shadows (`blur: 16px`, `opacity: 0.7`) to ensure legibility when downscaled to mobile feeds (300×168px).
