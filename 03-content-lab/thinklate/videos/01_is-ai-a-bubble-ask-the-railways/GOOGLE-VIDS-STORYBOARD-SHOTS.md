## 🎬 Visual Architecture: A-Roll vs. B-Roll Structure

* **A-Roll (On-Camera Host):** The **ThinkLate Owl Avatar** is NOT a floating corner bubble. It acts as the **full-screen documentary host** (chest-up studio framing, dark slate navy `#1B2A4A` backdrop, dramatic studio rim lighting, expressive linework). Whenever the narrator delivers direct personal observations, rhetorical questions, or channel axioms, cut full-frame to the host.
* **B-Roll (Cinematic World):** Dynamic 1080p cinematic AI footage generated in **Google Vids** or **Google Flow** (steam locomotives in rain, hyperscale server halls, frantic Victorian stock exchanges).
* **Forensic Evidence (Pop-outs):** Authentic primary documents (SSRN abstracts, Bank of England share archives, Goldman Sachs & Sequoia charts) sliding in over the B-roll as broadcast overlays.

## 🚀 How to Use These Prompts in Google Vids / Google Flow

1. Open **[vids.google.com](https://vids.google.com)** (or Google Flow video workspace).
2. Create a new project and select **16:9 Landscape (1080p)**.
3. For each scene:
   - Click **Add AI Scene / Generate Video** ➔ Paste the corresponding **Google Vids / Flow Prompt** above.
   - For **A-Roll scenes**: Upload `brand/00_MASTER_avatar.png` / `brand/expressions/E1_neutral.png` as the main on-camera speaker graphic.
   - For **Evidence scenes**: Insert the screenshots from `videos/01_is-ai-a-bubble-ask-the-railways/assets/` as floating overlay cards.
4. Import `audio_takes/ambient_drone.wav` onto the background audio track (set volume to 12–14%).
5. Record or paste your human voiceover takes onto the primary narration track.
