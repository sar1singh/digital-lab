# Google Vids — Video 1 Production Prompt Pack

> **Google Workspace AI Video Creation Guide**: Use this prompt pack inside [Google Vids](https://vids.google.com/) to auto-generate animated scenes, motion transitions, and B-roll for Video 1.

---

## 🎬 1. Master Style & Video Setup Prompt

Paste this into the Google Vids **"Help me create a video"** initial prompt box:

```
Create a 6-minute high-end documentary explainer video titled "Is AI a Bubble? Ask the Railways".
Visual Style: Premium cinematic documentary, 35mm film aesthetic, low-key lighting, deep slate navy (#1B2A4A) and warm amber (#D99A2B) accents.
Pacing: Measured, analytical, thought-provoking.
Camera Motion: Continuous slow push-in (Ken Burns 2% zoom) on all documents and stills. Never static.
Soundscape: Deep ambient documentary synthesizer drone with subtle low-frequency transition whooshes.
Tone: Grounded, conversational, clear English spoken by an intelligent tech professional. Zero corporate fluff.
```

## 🎞️ 2. Scene-by-Scene Storyboard & Motion Prompts for Google Vids

### Scene 1: The Cold Open (00:00 – 00:20)
* **Visual Prompt:** *"Wide cinematic exterior shot of a massive modern hyperscale AI data center at deep twilight dusk. Sleek slate navy industrial architecture, soft amber and blue cooling vent lights, light volumetric mist, wet pavement reflecting lights. Slow 2% camera push-in."*
* **Motion / Transition:** Slow push-in -> Cut to Scene 2 on the final beat.
* **On-Screen Text:** Clean cold open. Zero lower-thirds.
* **Spoken Voiceover:**
  > *"Your company has probably announced an AI budget too. Some new tool, some new platform, or some big 'AI-first' strategy. And nobody has really explained where the money is actually coming from. Or what happens, when it stops."*

### Scene 9: The Invariant Formula (03:48 – 04:06)

* **Visual Prompt:** *"Academic text excerpt analyzing the earlier 1830s railway mania with amber highlight on: 'collapsed prematurely, as projects started during its exuberant phase became successful'."*
* **Motion / Transition:** Camera slowly zooms into the word 'prematurely'.
* **Lower-Third Citation:**
  * Badge: `[ HISTORICAL PARADOX · FALSE BUBBLE ]`
  * Title: *"SSRN 1537338: The Difficulty of Identifying True Irrationality"*
* **Spoken Voiceover:**
  > *"And this is the part where I have to pause. Because there is another detail that makes this question genuinely difficult to answer. Except, Odlyzko writes that a few years later, people realized it had, quote, 'collapsed prematurely', because the projects started during that crazy phase actually succeeded in the long run. In other words, a bubble that turned out not to be irrational after all."*
