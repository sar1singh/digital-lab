# Video 02 Pre-Production Pack: The Global Sand Mafia
**Estimated Target Runtime:** 10-12 Minutes
**Tone:** Casual, sarcastic, conversational friend (Not a stiff corporate lecture).

---

## Phase 1: Title & Thumbnail Anchor
*   **Title:** The Illegal $150 Billion Industry No One Talks About
*   **Thumbnail Visual:** A massive, terrifying 2D Anime style sand dredger claw destroying a peaceful riverbed at night.
*   **Thumbnail Text:** THEY ARE STEALING DIRT.

---

## Phase 2: Script Outline (The 4 Acts)
1. **The Hook (0:00 - 1:30):** The Paradox. Sand is everywhere, so how is there a global shortage? Introduce the joke: we have so much desert sand, but desert sand is useless. You can't build with it.
2. **The Stakes & The Villain (1:30 - 4:00):** The Concrete Trap. Every single building, glass window, and microchip needs *river* sand. Introduce the "Sand Mafia" in India/Asia—violent cartels literally stealing beaches.
3. **The Mega-Structure / Conflict (4:00 - 8:30):** The Supply Chain. How stolen dirt gets laundered into luxury high-rises. The environmental collapse of rivers and the insane black market economics.
4. **The Resolution / Pattern (8:30 - 11:00):** The takeaway. Commodities are invisible until they run out. How to spot artificial scarcity in your own business ventures.

---

## Phase 3: Visual Element Planning (For Your Gemini Advanced Tomorrow)
*You only need these 4 massive canvases to cover the entire 11-minute video using DaVinci panning.*

### Canvas 1: The Useless Desert (For Act 1)
**Gemini Prompt:** *"A sweeping, 90s retro anime style wide landscape of the Sahara desert. High-res, clean, smooth animation style, no VHS grain. **Gemini Prompt:** *"A dark, moody, Investigator's Sketch style drawing in charcoal and pencil. A massive, terrifying industrial dredging ship illegally ripping up a riverbed at night. Deep shadows, highly detailed, gritty, sepia-toned textured paper, no text."*
**DaVinci Action:** Zoom in slowly on the dredging claw while you introduce the violence and money involved in the black market.

**DaVinci Action:** Hold static. Use DaVinci's native text tools to overlay the "$150 Billion" and "50 Billion Tons" numbers popping up as you speak.
