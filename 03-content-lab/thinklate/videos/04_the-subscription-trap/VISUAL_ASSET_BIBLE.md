## 🎨 1. The Cinematic Graphic Noir Aesthetic Suite

Instead of treating the screen as a generic B-roll wallpaper or a frantic 4-second stock montage, Video 04 uses **Cinematic Graphic Noir anchor frames**. Each scene is anchored by one sustained, atmospheric plate that lets the audience visualize the character and environment while the voiceover and diegetic Foley drive the emotional tension.

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
```

## 🎬 2. The 7-Scene Anchor Frame Architecture

### Scene 1: The $18 Heated Seat (00:00 – ~01:15)
* **Spoken Question:** *Why did a sixty-thousand-dollar car ask for eighteen dollars to warm a seat?*
* **Diegetic Audio Foley:** Wind howling $\rightarrow$ Heavy car door thuds shut $\rightarrow$ Seatbelt click $\rightarrow$ Absolute dead silence $\rightarrow$ Soft digital beep $\rightarrow$ Dull refusal buzz.
* **Anchor Plate 01: Frosted Luxury Cockpit at Dawn**
  * **File:** `assets/anchor_01_frosted_dashboard.jpg`
  * **Visual Content:** Frosted windshield cutting cold dawn light, extreme close-up of a high-end luxury vehicle touchscreen glowing with an alert: *"HEATED SEAT INACTIVE. SUBSCRIBE FOR £15/MONTH"*. Dark leather steering wheel in foreground.
  * **Camera Motion:** 3% slow push-in over the entire 72-second scene.
  * **Gemini Nano Prompt:**
    > `Cinematic wide 16:9 graphic noir illustration of a luxury modern electric car interior on a freezing dawn morning. Chiaroscuro lighting, cold blue tones cutting through heavy frosted glass on the windshield. Extreme close-up of the center console digital touchscreen glowing brightly with a crisp dark slate notification card reading 'HEATED SEAT INACTIVE. SUBSCRIBE FOR £15/MONTH'. Deep studio slate navy #080C14, graphic novel realism, moody atmospheric shadows, 35mm film grain, 8k resolution, zero flat vector look, zero cartoon.`
