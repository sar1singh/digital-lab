## 🔍 Detailed Evidence Dossiers

### 1. BMW Heated Seat Subscription & U-Turn
- **The Launch:** July 2022. Featured in UK, Germany, South Korea, and South Africa ConnectedDrive stores.
- **The Physical Truth:** The seat coils, wiring harness, and relays were pre-installed in all vehicles at the plant to reduce manufacturing SKU complexity. The subscription was purely a software lockout.
- **The U-Turn:** In September 2023, BMW head of sales Pieter Nota confirmed to *Autocar* at IAA Mobility Munich that BMW discontinued hardware subscriptions. Quote: *"People feel that they paid double – which was actually not true, but perception is reality, I always say. So that was the reason we stopped that."*
- **Primary Links:**
  - [Business Insider Report](https://www.businessinsider.com/bmw-subscription-features-heated-seats-steering-5-series-2022-7)
  - [Forbes Autocar Interview Report](https://www.forbes.com/sites/alistaircharlton/2023/09/07/bmw-drops-controversial-heated-seats-subscription-to-refocus-on-software-services/)

### 2. HP "Dynamic Security" & Enrique Lores Interview
- **The Mechanism:** Firmware pushed silently via Wi-Fi from 2016 onwards that identifies chips on third-party ink cartridges and blocks printing.
- **Regulatory Penalties:** Italian AGCM fined HP €10,000,000 on Dec 9, 2020 (Decision PS11144). Euroconsumers settled in Sept 2022 across Belgium, Spain, Italy, and Portugal with a compensation fund of up to $1.35 million.
- **The Quote:** HP CEO Enrique Lores on CNBC Squawk Box (Jan 2024):
  > *"Every time a customer buys a printer, it is an investment for us. We are investing on that customer, and if this customer doesn't print enough or doesn't use our supplies, it's a bad investment."*
- **Primary Links:**
  - [AGCM Press Release PS11144](https://en.agcm.it/en/media/press-releases/2020/12/PS11144)
  - [Euroconsumers Dynamic Security Settlement](https://www.euroconsumers.org/hp-and-euroconsumers-settle-on-dynamic-security/)

### 3. John Deere, Nebraska Testimony & Ukrainian Pirate Firmware
- **The Vice Investigation:** Jason Koebler's March 2017 *Vice Motherboard* report titled *"Why American Farmers Are Hacking Their Tractors With Ukrainian Firmware"*.
- **The Sworn Testimony:** Danny Kluthe, a hog farmer in Dodge County, Nebraska, testified to the Nebraska State Legislature that dealers charge $230 just to drive out to the farm and $130 an hour while a technician connects a laptop to clear error codes and authorize parts.
- **The Hackers:** Farmers turned to Polish and Ukrainian hacking forums (such as *Car-Masters.co*) to purchase cracked copies of John Deere Service Advisor software and payload keys.
- **Script Refinement:** We removed fictional dramatizations ($30 sensor, 2 mph limp mode, "frost on harvest forecast") and anchored the script strictly in Danny Kluthe's verified legislative record and the real fee structure.
- **Primary Link:**

### 4. Wall Street Valuation Multiples (Software vs Hardware)
- **Hardware Valuation:** Typically 1–2x annual sales (P/S ratio) due to inventory capital intensity, supply chain risk, and cyclical demand.
- **Software/SaaS ARR Multiple:** According to the **SaaS Capital Index (SCI)**, the median public SaaS company ARR multiple stood at **6.7x** as of June 2025. While 2021 pandemic peak saw upper-quartile valuations hit 20x–25x, modern normalized markets price recurring revenue at 6x–8x.
- **Script Refinement:** Replaced sensationalized "10x, 12x, even 20x" text with "six, seven, sometimes far more". Re-rendered `assets/CHART_VALUATION_MULTIPLIER_animated.mp4` to display 1.5x hardware ($1.5B) vs 6.7x SaaS ($6.7B) with a permanent source footnote.
- **Primary Link:**

### 5. Phoebus Cartel & The Centennial Light
- **Phoebus Agreement:** Executives from Osram, Philips, and General Electric convened in Geneva, Switzerland, in December 1924, officially incorporating *Phœbus S.A.* on January 15, 1925.
- **The 1,000-Hour Cap:** Manufacturers engineered filaments to shorten lifespan from 2,500 hours to 1,000 hours. Sample bulbs were tested in a Geneva lab; manufacturers exceeding 1,000 hours were fined according to a graduated tariff in Swiss francs.
- **The Centennial Light:** Hand-blown by Shelby Electric Company in Ohio in the late 1890s, donated to Livermore Fire Department in 1901. Certified by Guinness World Records as the world's longest-burning lightbulb.
- **Primary Links:**
  - [Phoebus Cartel (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phoebus_cartel)
  - [Centennial Bulb Official Record](https://www.centennialbulb.org/)

### 6. Indian Smartphone Loan Remote Locking & RBI Regulations
- **The Mechanism:** Indian NBFCs and fintechs install mobile device management (MDM) software—including Google Device Lock Controller and Samsung Knox Guard—at the point of sale. If an Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) is overdue, the phone screen is remotely restricted.
- **Impact on Gig Workers:** Gig workers (Zomato, Swiggy, Rapido, Uber) rely on smartphones as income infrastructure. Bricking the screen locks them out of delivery apps, creating an involuntary default loop.
- **Regulatory Framework:** In 2024, the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) cracked down on predatory device-locking practices, enforcing guidelines requiring clear borrower consent, preserving access to emergency calling, and mandating prompt unlocks upon payment.
- **Primary Links:**

### 7. FTC v. Adobe Inc. (Federal Lawsuit, June 2024)
- **Federal Complaint:** FTC sued Adobe and executives Maninder Sawhney and David Wadhwani in US District Court (ND Cal) on June 17, 2024 (Case 3:24-cv-03530).
- **The Charges:** Deceptive pre-selection of the "Annual Paid Monthly" (APM) subscription without prominent disclosure of the 50% early termination fee, alongside an administrative obstacle course for cancellation.
- **Primary Link:**
  - [FTC Official Press Release (June 17, 2024)](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/06/ftc-takes-action-against-adobe-executives-hiding-fees-preventing-consumers-easily-cancelling)

### 8. Right-to-Repair Laws: Colorado, California, Minnesota
- **Colorado HB23-1011:** *Consumer Right to Repair Agricultural Equipment Act*, signed into law by Governor Jared Polis on April 25, 2023. First US law explicitly guaranteeing farmers diagnostic tools, software, and parts for agricultural machinery.
- **California SB 244 & Minnesota DFRA:** Landmark electronics right-to-repair bills passed in 2023. Both explicitly **exempt** agricultural equipment and motor vehicles.
- **Primary Links:**
