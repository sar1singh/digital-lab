## What the channel is

- **Niche:** how work, money and power actually function — economics, geopolitics, technology — traced down to the viewer's own working life
- **Category:** Education / business & economics explainer
- **Language:** **Hinglish narration, English packaging** (titles, thumbnails, on-screen text, charts, description, subtitles)
- **Audience:** Indian knowledge workers 22–40, considering or already doing independent work for global clients
- **Lens:** who decided this, what were they optimising for, why is it still shaped that way, how does it reach your life
- **Method:** documented history → the pattern → something happening now → the chain to the individual, **every link labelled** DOCUMENTED / LIKELY / PLAUSIBLE / SPECULATIVE
- **Format:** faceless, my own voice · Floor 6–8 min / Standard 14–18 / Pillar 25–30 · **2–3 videos per fortnight through video 12** (revised 2026-09-13 from fortnightly), mostly Floor tier

## Out of scope — do not propose

Finance, investing or trading advice (SEBI rules + YouTube's AI-narration ban) · automotive and dealership (my employer) · medical, legal, health advice · top-10s, listicles, compilations · news commentary · true crime · plain history narration · self-help and "how to succeed" · YouTube-meta content as a pillar · **AI-generated illustrative imagery in editorial content**

## Hard rules

1. **Never state a figure not present in a source you actually opened.** Mark anything unsourced `[VERIFY]`
2. **Label every causal-chain link** by confidence. Never present an inference at a documented link's confidence
3. **Anchor every chain in the financial or career domain.** Social and psychological effects sit on top, marked as inference
4. **Cover the invariant, not the instance.** A title with a product name or version number gets rewritten
5. **Offer a pattern to notice, never an action to take.** Explaining why is analysis; telling someone what to do is advice, and advice is out of scope
6. **No AI-generated illustrative imagery, no fake archival material, no AI voice.** Real charts, real documents, real archive
7. **Never end on a prediction.** End on a better question
8. **On-screen text complements narration, never duplicates it**
9. **Automate assembly, never judgement**
