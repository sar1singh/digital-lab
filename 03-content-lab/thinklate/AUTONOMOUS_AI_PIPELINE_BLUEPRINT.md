### Phase 1: Outlier Topic Selection (Banning Academic Syllabuses)
- **The Core Rule:** A video topic is NEVER a history lecture or textbook chapter. It is an **active systemic scandal affecting 50M+ people right now**.
- **The 3-Factor Outlier Formula (Infographics Show & Incognito Benchmark):**
  1. *Mass Everyday Pain:* Jobs, rent, groceries, car subscriptions, AI replacement, tech obsolescence.
  2. *Identifiable Corporate Antagonist:* Wall Street private equity, Big Tech monopolists, pharmaceutical cartels.
  3. *High-Stakes Moral Outrage:* Everyday honest people following rules get fleeced while rule-breakers get multi-million dollar payouts.
