### Phase 1: Outlier Topic Selection (Banning Academic Syllabuses)
- **The Core Rule:** A video topic is NEVER a history lecture or textbook chapter. It is an **active systemic scandal affecting 50M+ people right now**.
- **The 3-Factor Outlier Formula (Infographics Show & Incognito Benchmark):**
  1. *Mass Everyday Pain:* Jobs, rent, groceries, car subscriptions, AI replacement, tech obsolescence.
  2. *Identifiable Corporate Antagonist:* Wall Street private equity, Big Tech monopolists, pharmaceutical cartels.
  3. *High-Stakes Moral Outrage:* Everyday honest people following rules get fleeced while rule-breakers get multi-million dollar payouts.

---

### Phase 2: High-CTR Packaging Matrix (The 150ms Mobile Test)
- **Deliverable:** 3 titles ($\le 50$ characters, mobile-safe) paired with 3 distinct 1280x720 thumbnail hypotheses for YouTube's native *Test & Compare* engine.
- **The 3 Faceless Outlier Design Principles:**
  1. *The Infographics Show Scale Rule:* Small relatable consumer vs. giant menacing institutional machine.
  2. *The Incognito Satirical Villain Rule:* Smug corporate villain in sunglasses with physical connection to the victim (laser kill-switch, cash vacuum, golden key).
  3. *The Alux Hierarchy Rule:* Clean focal point, 0 dark muddy clutter, high contrast against YouTube's `#0F0F0F` dark mode.
- **Typography Standard:** Swiss Brutalist Two-Tone (chalk white + acid neon-lime `#E2FF00`) with directional depth shadows. Zero 2012 meme font, zero balloon foil.
- **The Lean Sandbox Protocol:** Prototype in free Gemini Nano at zero cost $\rightarrow$ 1-shot production render in Ideogram/Leonardo.

---

### Phase 3: Sumeet Jain "Roast & Reveal" Scripting & HimanshuG Pacing
- **Tone:** A witty, sharp friend roasting corporate absurdities over drinks. Never a professor.
- **The 0–10s Retention Contract:** The physical scandal promised in the title/thumbnail MUST explode on screen in the first 5 seconds.
- **The 4-Step Roast & Reveal Loop:**
  $$\text{Corporate PR Claim} \longrightarrow \text{Blunt Street Translation} \longrightarrow \text{Absurdity Roast} \longrightarrow \text{Wall Street Financial Reveal}$$
- **HimanshuG High-Low Pacing:** Every 45–60 seconds, schedule an emotional peak (outrageous quote, shocking multiplier) followed by a 1.5s dead-air drop.

---

### Phase 4: Automated Adversarial QC Gate (Zero Reliance on User Checks)
Before recording or voiceover synthesis, the script must autonomously pass:
1. `audit_story_script.py`: 0 syllabus markers, 0 teacher clichés, max 12 words per sentence clause.
2. `fact_check_qc.py`: 100% of claims grounded in verified regulatory/primary URLs in `SOURCES_VERIFICATION.md`.
3. `counter_agents.py`: 4-agent Council testing narrative tension, cynical viewer drop-off, and audio Foley presence.

---

### Phase 5: Voiceover Engine & Studio Sharp DSP Chain
- **Primary Voice:** `en-US-ChristopherNeural` via `uvx edge-tts` (`--rate=+7%`, `--pitch=+0Hz`) @ 148 WPM.
- **Cost:** **$0.00 perpetually** (unlimited characters, zero credit caps).
- **DSP Chain:** High-pass at 80 Hz $\rightarrow$ -3dB EQ notch at 220 Hz $\rightarrow$ +2.5dB air boost at 3.5 kHz $\rightarrow$ Optical compression (2.5:1) $\rightarrow$ BS.1770-4 normalization to **-16.1 LUFS**.
- **Syntax:** Controlled by natural punctuation (ellipses `...` for comedic timing, em-dashes `—` for inflection shifts).

---

### Phase 6: Whisper-First Timeline Assembly
- **Ground Truth Law:** Timeline cues (`build_timeline_cues.py`) MUST parse speech boundaries directly from `transcription_timestamps.json`. Theoretical estimates are banned.
- **Visuals:** 100% contextual documentary stock footage or programmatic charts. Zero gameplay filler on Video Track 1.
- **Rendering:** Headless GPU NVENC rendering (<4 minutes render time).

---

### Phase 7: Post-Publishing Verification & Analytics Intelligence
- Autonomously audit published YouTube streams via `youtube-publish-qc` (1080p transcode, 7+ clickable chapters, description integrity).
- Direct YouTube Analytics API monitoring via `tools/fetch_yt_analytics.py` to track retention and traffic sources over time.
- Autonomous persistent sync to `PROFILE.md` and `lifeos-hq/00-control/DAILY_JOURNAL.md`.
