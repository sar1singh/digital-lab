## 0. The 3-Step "Zero-Blank" Recording Workflow

Never sit down to record blank. Use this exact sequence before opening OBS:

1. **Step 1 — Pick 1 Lead Episode Idea + 1 Textless Thumbnail First (`Section 1`):** Choose which main hook will drive the **Title + Thumbnail** (remember: thumbnails can be 100% real-world / non-game metaphors). Pay off or tease that lead hook inside the **first 30 seconds** (`0:00–0:30` promise bridge) as soon as gameplay begins.
2. **Step 2 — Open 2–3 "Refresher Pods" on Your Phone or 2nd Monitor (`Section 2`):** Each pod fits in 4 ultra-short lines so you can glance at it in 5 seconds during ocean sailing, horse riding, or quiet stealth traversal without losing flow.
3. **Step 3 — Rollover Remaining Pods to Part 7 / Part 8:** Never force all topics into one video. When combat or a cutscene hits, stop talking naturally (*"Ruko pehle is ship ko todte hain, phir batata hoon..."*), let game audio breathe for 5–10 seconds, and mark `[x]` on completed pods after recording so unused pods carry forward automatically.
