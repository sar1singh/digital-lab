## 2. TIER 1 — UNIVERSAL MANDATORY LAWS (Every Video, Both Channels)

These 8 practices recur consistently across 4–7 videos of the corpus. **No ThinkLate (`@wethinklate`) or Off Script (`@OffScriptEddy`) video may ship without passing all 8:**

1. **U1 — Pre-Production 4-Scenario TAM + Outlier + Relatability Gate (`2YnT` + `U8R` + `6i1Z`):**
   - Run live `search_web` / YouTube verification (`>= 5/6` gates) *before* scripting — even for pre-decided vault ideas.
   - Check the **4 Scenarios (`U8R` `07:31–09:12`):** `>300K–500K` views in top 3–6 results (`<= 12 mo`) + `>= 3x–5x` channel-average outlier + distributed creators (`>90%` Green) OR monopolized giants flipped by a fresh angle (`>70%`). Never ship Low TAM (`<20K`) + Low Outlier (`0%` Kill).
   - **Cold-Start & Uniqueness Formula (`6i1Z` `07:28`, `09:14`):** Escape `Item Cold-Start` (*Covington et al. 2016*) with $\text{Uniqueness} = (\text{Perspective} \times \text{Subversion}) + \text{Voice} + \text{Format}$. Stay **similar to your audience** (relatable everyday lens) while looking **Von Restorff distinct from the 20 competitors next to you**.
2. **U2 — `<= 2–3` Focal Points, MIT `0.5s` Glanceability & Competitor-Grade Art (`9_7q` + Phase 2):**
   - Maximum **2–3 focal points** arranged along a **Rule of Thirds / Left-to-Right Saccadic Zigzag** (`13ms` gist $\rightarrow$ `17–50ms` complexity rejection $\rightarrow$ `100–150ms` emotion $\rightarrow$ `300–500ms` click).
   - **Banned Permanently:** Sterile HTML/CSS cards, clip-art SVG icons, flat charts, posterize filters, and dark-on-dark blends. Use real high-contrast photography, visceral oversized physical props, bright rim lighting, and Complementary / Split-Complementary contrast visible in **both Light Mode (`#FFFFFF`) and Dark Mode (`#0F0F0F`)**.
3. **U3 — `1+1=3` Informational Asymmetry (`9_7q` + `2YnT` + `fObs`):**
   - **Zero word overlap between Title and Thumbnail (`1+1=1` error).** Title frames the searchable/conceptual stakes; Thumbnail shows the visceral consequence, physical prop (`Zero1` chai + ₹500), freeze-frame panic (`BeastBoyShub`), or 1–3 word punchline.
4. **U4 — `I + C + C` First-3-Second Hook & `< 30s` Payoff Contract (`fPfP` + `wXl8` + `9_7q` + `U8R`):**
   - Open inside `0:00–0:03` using **Information Gap** (*Tease Insight / Challenge Belief / Withhold Payoff / Curiosity Reward*) + **Contrast** (2 opposing facts in `2.5s` $\rightarrow$ Orienting Response) + **Creativity** (*unexpected object pairing like PK's helmet*), synced simultaneously via **Say It + Show It + Write It + Pre-Emptive J-cut SFX**.
   - Explicitly advance or pay off the **Title + Thumbnail promise inside `0:00–0:30`** (eliminates `> 50%` 30s drop-off; `30–40%` attrition is the normal buffer). Zero *"Hey guys / channel intro"* preamble.
5. **U5 — Viral Car Engine: 4-Piece Zeigarnik + `4s` Story-Context Cuts + Intentional Silences (`wXl8` + `fObs`):**
   - **Engine (`wXl8`):** Combine *Information Gap*, *Pattern Disruption* (`C-B-A` climax first or `A-B----` cut before consequence), *Cognitive Dissonance* (`Common Good Habit + Negative Result`), and raw *Emotional Suspension* (unpolished candid reaction before reveal).
   - **Interior:** Escalate Stakes + **Hitchcock's Bomb Under the Table** (*Suspense > Surprise*).
   - **Exterior (`fObs`):** Visual shifts every **3–4 seconds in context of the *unfolding story*** (never word-by-word wallpaper assembly), punctuated by **`SR Pay`-style intentional silences** and `"But / Therefore"` mirror twists (`15–25s` background context pivot $\rightarrow$ mid-loop $\rightarrow$ payoff). Must pass the **Blindfold Test**.
6. **U6 — Real Archives Over 100% AI Slop (`fObs` `06:00` + `2YnT`):**
   - **"Using Real Archives (Not fully Ai Gen)":** Anchor sequences in primary filings, receipts, court dockets, real UI screenshots, historical press photos, real gameplay footage, and custom pixel/diagram UIs (`SELECT YOUR PLAYER`). In the AI era, **human editorial craft is the luxury moat (`10:24`)**.
7. **U7 — 1st-Person "Friend-Not-Boss" Conversational Authority (`fObs` + `wXl8` + `6i1Z`):**
   - Ban 3rd-person lecture detachment (*"Studies show..."*). Speak as a smart friend in the room (*"After 40 hours digging into records, I found..."* with compact lower-third source credit at `13:25`, or *"Middle-Class Dad's Glasses"* relatability at `09:40`) with `Slayy Pop` / pre-face-reveal `Techno Gamerz` vocal modulation.
8. **U8 — Series IP & Sequenced Multi-Form Repurposing (`2YnT` + `U8R`):**
   - Protect format consistency, recurring hosts (`Eddy` / `Vera`), 1-line **Open Loops** (`Shan Prasher`), and **"Smart Room" takeaways** so **New (`~14%`) / Casual (`~64%`) / Regular (`~22%`)** viewers compound into returning fans. Repurpose by **re-sequencing story arcs** across Shorts and text carousels — never blind VidIQ clip-dumps.
