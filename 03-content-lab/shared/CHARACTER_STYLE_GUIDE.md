## 1. Visual Anatomy & Physical Anchors

| Feature | Canonical Specification | Strict Bans |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Head** | Stylized pure white cartoon owl head with clean, thick black marker ink contours; two distinct small pointed ear tufts at the top corners. | No realistic bird feathers, no textured photorealistic owl fur, no 3D shading. |
| **Eyewear** | Thin black circular wireframe reading spectacles over large, round expressive cartoon eyes with dark pupils. | No thick hipster frames, no colored rims, no sunglasses. |
| **Beak & Expression** | Small triangular yellow/amber beak. Open wide during shock/shouting, subtle sly smirk when scheming. | No realistic hooked raptor beak. |
| **Neck Accent** | Thin black collar holding a small, polished golden round medallion / pendant (`#C5A059`). | No ruffly clown collars, no ties (in this 2D doodle format). |
| **Body** | Minimalist black ink stick figure: simple straight line torso, thin stick arms, stick legs, and L-shaped stick feet. | No muscular limbs, no bulky clothing, no 3D cylindrical geometry. |
| **Art Style** | 2D minimalist cartoon doodle cel art in the tradition of *OverSimplified*, *Casually Explained*, and *Monkey Explains*. Thick ink outlines, flat white fills. | Zero 3D CGI, zero photorealism, zero gradients, zero airbrush shadows. |

## 3. The 10 Canonical Emotional Poses (Reference Index)

Referenced directly from `eddy_official_style_reference.jpg`:

1. **CURIOUS:** Hand on chin/beak, other hand on hip, head tilted slightly.
2. **SHOCKED:** Glasses flying off face, mouth wide open in scream, jaw dropped, bulging eyes, sweat droplets flying.
3. **FURIOUS POINTING:** Eyebrows angled down aggressively, beak open shouting, right stick arm pointing forward with index finger, wide lunge stance.
4. **LAUGHING / WOOHOO:** Crescent-closed eyes, laughing beak, arms thrown wide up in the air, jumping on one leg.
5. **PUZZLED:** Hand scratching behind ear tuft, head tilted sideways, one eyebrow raised, `?` floating.
6. **TIRED / SLUMPED:** Head drooping, droopy eyelid bags, arms hanging limply down at sides, exhausted posture.
7. **SCHEMING / THINKING:** Evil mastermind pose; stick hands tented/rubbing together, cunning half-lidded smirk, knees bent sneaking.
8. **SMUG:** Arms crossed over chest, confident half-lidded smirk, leaning back slightly.
9. **TERRIFIED:** Hands clutching cheeks ("Home Alone"), mouth open in horror, knocking knees with tremor lines.
10. **TRIUMPHANT:** Hands planted firmly on hips, chest puffed out, sparkling glints `✦` on both sides.

## 4. Google Flow Image-to-Video Animation Protocol

To protect founder credits and prevent AI distortion, every Google Flow Veo animation prompt MUST adhere to these 3 laws:

1. **Camera Lock Law:** Always append `static locked camera, zero camera movement, smooth 24fps 2D hand-drawn animation physics`. Never allow the AI video engine to orbit, zoom uncontrollably, or simulate 3D camera pan.
2. **Single Motion Law:** Direct the character to perform ONE clear physical action per 4-second clip (e.g., *blinks and scratches head*, or *jabs index finger forward twice*, or *trembles in shock as receipt flutters*).
3. **No 3D Morphing:** Explicitly instruct `maintain 2D cartoon physics, flat cel animation, zero 3D deformation`.
