## 🎯 Key Packaging Insights & Strategic Rules

1. **Mobile Title Truncation Check:** Videos with titles $\le$ 50 characters retain full visibility on the YouTube mobile app without getting cut off with ellipses (`...`).
2. **Dark Mode Inversion Principle:** YouTube's dark mode UI is `#0F0F0F`. Thumbnails with low luminance (< 50) and low saturation blend into the feed and lose up to 60% of baseline CTR. High-contrast saturations (Acid Lime, Vivid Orange, White cutout text) score $\ge 65$ and reliably command the 150ms scroll glance.
3. **Recommended Visual Architecture:**
   - **Left:** High-expression character portrait or dramatic conflict object.
   - **Right:** 2-3 words Swiss Brutalist block typography in high-contrast two-tone.
   - **Background:** Saturated, high-clarity graphic environment (avoiding muddy black shadows).
