## 1. Executive Purpose & Workspace Hierarchy

`digital-lab/` is the dedicated execution repository for **all practical digital businesses, commercial digital products, and digital media channels (YouTube, social distribution)**.

### Workspace Role Division:
- **`lifeos-hq/` (Broader, Generic Place):** Personal OS (health, fitness, habits, personal finance tracker overview, personal creator reflections). Contains the high-level cognitive graph (`lifeos-hq/00-control/MIND/GRAPH.md`) capturing founder thought nodes and macro visions.
- **`digital-lab/` (Concrete Commercial Execution):** Holds all production code, scripts, listing assets, design systems, and commercial pipelines for every digital venture.

## 3. ThinkLate Long-Term Vision: The Tri-Pillar Media Company

ThinkLate (`@wethinklate`) is structured around **three compounding long-term pillars** (operating as flagship umbrella or dedicated sub-channels):

1. **🎓 Infotainment (Flagship Explainer Channel):**
   - High-retention deep explainers on economics, sovereign debt, cognitive models, and system paradoxes.
   - Distinctive anime owl host providing intellectual wit, memorable visual anchors, and anti-boring pacing.
2. **🎨 Animation Studio (Anime Owl Universe & Narrative Shorts):**
   - 2D character animation, expressive posture systems, and visual metaphors.
   - Dedicated animation shorts sub-channel / social series expanding the anime owl lore into serialized philosophical storytelling.
3. **💼 Media Agency (B2B High-Retention Visual Storytelling):**
   - Commercial production arm offering high-retention explainer video production, script doctoring, and animated infographic systems for tech, fintech, and sovereign infrastructure companies.

## 5. Primary Directives & Governance
1. **First Revenue & Compound Utility:** Deliver practical value into the real world. Avoid speculative overhead.
2. **Hard $0.00 Operational Spend:** Default to local compute (Whisper.cpp, ffmpeg, local Git) and free cloud tiers (OpenRouter Free DeepSeek, Cloudflare R2 10 GB free tier).
3. **Agent-OS Scoped Routing:** All agent prompts regarding YouTube, digital products, Gumroad, anime owl animation, or media production route directly to `digital-lab/`.
